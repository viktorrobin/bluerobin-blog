## What it taught me

The default should be **trust**: assume things are fine unless someone raises a flag — *no news is
good news* — and invest in the conditions that make exceptions surface on their own. Status-chasing
is a tax you pay for the absence of trust and visibility. Larson's emphasis on autonomy and using
1:1s for growth (not status) is the same idea.

## What I changed

- Shifted from status updates to **outcome ownership** plus lightweight async signals (dashboards).
- Reclaimed 1:1s for growth and unblocking, not progress reports.
- Made escalation explicit: clear norms for *when* to raise a flag, so silence genuinely meant fine.
