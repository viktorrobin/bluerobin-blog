## What it taught me

Cross-team work fails at the **seams**, so you manage the seams: map dependencies explicitly, then
sequence the work into **incremental milestones that each integrate and deliver something
verifiable**. Aligning teams on the *increments* (and the dependency order) beats aligning them on a
shared deadline. This is the same incremental-over-big-bang logic as migrations, applied to delivery.

## What I changed

- Built an explicit dependency map across the teams before committing a date.
- Sequenced into incremental milestones, each with a demoable, integrated slice that retired risk.
- Aligned teams on a shared milestone tracker showing dependencies — not just a final date.
