## What it taught me

Culture isn't the values on the wall; it's the worst behaviour you tolerate from your best people.
The "brilliant jerk" trade is negative once you count the suppressed ideas and the people who go
quiet. Larson is clear that psychological safety is a precondition for high-performing teams, not a
nicety.

## What I changed

- Named the behavioural norms explicitly, and acted on the *first* clear violation, not the tenth.
- Made collaboration a real signal in hiring and performance, not just delivery.
- Built routine, low-stakes feedback so problems surfaced small.
