## What it taught me

Throughput is governed by work-in-progress, not by how much you start (Little's Law makes this
concrete). Every parallel thread adds context-switching and coordination cost; **finishing beats
starting**. This is systems thinking applied to delivery — manage the constraint, not the activity.

## What I changed

- Set explicit WIP limits per person/team and enforced *finish-before-start*.
- Cut the number of concurrent initiatives; swarmed to push items over the line.
- Tracked cycle time and aging work-items instead of how much was started.
