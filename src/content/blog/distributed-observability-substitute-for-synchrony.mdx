## What it taught me

Larson frames shared context as the glue for distributed teams — but I'd assumed context had to
be delivered synchronously. It doesn't. Most of what that meeting conveyed (what shipped, what's
failing, who's over their error budget) is **observable**, and observability is cheaper, more
accurate, and available to everyone regardless of time zone.
