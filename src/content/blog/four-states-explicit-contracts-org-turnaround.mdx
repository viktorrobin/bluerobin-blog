## What it taught me

Larson's four states — *falling behind, treading water, repaying debt, innovating* — look
like a capacity question and are usually a **clarity** question. You can't add your way out of
treading water; you have to name the boundaries first. Adding people to an unclear system
mostly adds coordination cost.
