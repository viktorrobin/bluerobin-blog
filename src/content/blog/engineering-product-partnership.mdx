## What it taught me

The healthiest setup isn't a clean handoff; it's **shared ownership of the outcome**. Engineering
that only executes can't push back on the wrong problem, and product that decides alone loses the
feasibility and leverage engineers can see. Larson's framing of influence and partnering with
stakeholders applies here: you earn a seat in the problem definition, not just the solution.

## What I changed

- Engineers in discovery early — co-framing the problem and the success metric, not just sizing.
- One shared roadmap and one shared definition of done, owned jointly with product.
- A standing forum to trade context (constraints, leverage, risk) before commitments were made.
