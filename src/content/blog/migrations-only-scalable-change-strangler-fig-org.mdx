## What it taught me

Larson's line stuck with me: migrations are the only scalable way to manage change as code and
organisations grow. Rewrites and big-bang cutovers concentrate all the risk into one moment;
migrations spread it out and keep the business running. The same is true of *organisational*
change — process, structure, culture — which I'd been treating as something you announce rather
than migrate.
